Hello App Review team,

Thank you for reviewing MYEZ. Our answers follow in your order and are also in the App Review Notes field.

1. Screen recording: attached. It was recorded on an iPhone on the latest iOS with the submitted build 1.0.0 (2) from TestFlight. It begins at app launch and shows: signing in with an email account; a typed comparison with the one-time AI consent sheet and the result; a photo comparison; the History tab; logging out; registering an account and its first-run questionnaire; and deleting that account in the app, which returns to the sign-in screen. MYEZ has no feed, comments, chat, public profiles or paid content; the Share button only passes a private link to the user's own comparison to an app the user picks (an invite can show the sender's display name), so there is no reporting, blocking or purchase flow.

2. Purpose and audience: MYEZ is a free shopping decision aid, in English and Arabic, for shoppers aged 13 and over in Bahrain and the Gulf. It replaces the many searches needed to compare two products' specs, reviews and prices. The user types, photographs or links two products; MYEZ gathers public review snippets and retailer prices; AI compiles specs and writes one comparison: the recommended product, the reasons, where the other product wins, and each product's price when one is found.

3. Setup and access: the app opens on the sign-in screen. Please sign in with the user name and password in the Sign-In Information fields of App Review Information. It has completed the first-run questionnaire and has a raised daily limit. To test registration and deletion, please create a new account so the review account stays usable: tap Sign Up, enter any email and a password of 10+ characters (upper case, lower case, a digit) in Password and Confirm Password, tick "I am 13 or older and I agree to the Terms and Privacy" and tap Create Account. No email confirmation is needed. To compare: MYEZ tab > Type > two products (e.g. "iPhone 15" and "Samsung Galaxy S24") > Compare > "Agree and continue" on the one-time sheet. A comparison usually takes 20-35 seconds. An optional "Tell us about you" or notifications sheet may follow a result; "Skip for now" or "Not now" closes it. Delete account: Profile tab > gear icon (top right) > Delete account (under Account actions) > Delete account.

4. External services: OpenAI API (identifies products, extracts specs, prices and review summaries, writes the comparison, moderates output); Supabase (database, email sign-in); Sign in with Apple; Google Sign-In; Railway (hosting); Upstash (cache, usage counters); Serper (web and shopping search); public retailer websites and store search services, fetched by our servers, with Firecrawl and Scrape.do as fallbacks; Expo (app updates, notifications via APNs); Sentry (crash reports). No payment processor, advertising network or third-party analytics SDK is used.

5. Regions: MYEZ is offered on six App Store storefronts: Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates. The features are identical in all six. Content is centred on Bahrain: typed and photo comparisons search prices for Bahrain and show them in Bahraini Dinar (BHD) for all users, whatever country they pick; a price found only on an international listing is converted to BHD and labelled as converted. Link mode uses the linked page's price.

6. Regulation and third-party material: MYEZ does not operate in a regulated industry. It has no sales, payments, marketplace or affiliate links; its Terms state that it is not medical, health, financial or professional advice. It shows product names, retailer prices, AI-compiled specs, a summary of review praise (no quotes or source names) and star ratings only when a source publishes one (never AI-generated). Product images load directly from the websites that publish them or a search engine's image server; we do not host or resell them. Brand names only identify products.
