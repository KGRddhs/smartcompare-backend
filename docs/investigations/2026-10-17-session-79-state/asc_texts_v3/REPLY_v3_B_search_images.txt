Hello App Review team,

Thank you for reviewing MYEZ. Our answers follow in your order and are also in the App Review Notes field.

1. Screen recording: attached. It was recorded on an iPhone on the latest iOS with the submitted build 1.0.0 (3) from TestFlight. It begins at app launch and shows: signing in with an email account; a typed comparison with the one-time AI consent sheet and the result; a photo comparison; the History tab; logging out; registering an account and its first-run questionnaire; and deleting that account in the app, which returns to the sign-in screen. MYEZ has no feed, comments, chat, public profiles or paid content, so there is no reporting, blocking or purchase flow. Share sends a text summary of a new result, or, from History, a link to that comparison plus the user's invite code; when an invited friend signs up and runs a first comparison, the sender gets 5 bonus comparisons for 7 days (at most 3 friends, no cash value).

2. Purpose and audience: MYEZ is a free shopping decision aid, in English and Arabic, for shoppers aged 13 and over in Bahrain and the Gulf. It replaces many searches for specs, reviews and prices. The user types, photographs or links two products; MYEZ gathers public review snippets and retailer prices; AI compiles specs and writes one comparison: the recommended product, the reasons, where the other product wins, and each product's price when one is found.

3. Setup and access: the app opens on the sign-in screen. An account is required because MYEZ is account-based: each verdict is personalised to the priorities and budget saved in it, History is kept per account, and the daily allowance covering the paid AI and search requests is per account. Please sign in with the account in the Sign-In Information fields; we raised its daily limit for review (MYEZ sells nothing; no user can buy a higher limit). To test registration and deletion, please create a new account: tap Sign Up, enter any email and a 10+ character password (upper case, lower case, a digit) in Password and Confirm Password, tick "I am 13 or older and I agree to the Terms and Privacy" and tap Create Account. No email confirmation needed. To compare: MYEZ tab > Type > "iPhone 15" and "Samsung Galaxy S24" > Compare > "Agree and continue". A comparison usually takes 20-35 seconds. Delete account: Profile tab > gear icon (top right) > Delete account (under Account actions) > Delete account.

4. External services: OpenAI API (identifies products, extracts data, writes and moderates the comparison); Supabase (database, email sign-in); Sign in with Apple; Google Sign-In; Railway (hosting); Upstash (cache); Serper (web and shopping search); retailer websites and store search, fetched by our servers, with Firecrawl and Scrape.do as fallbacks; Expo (updates, push via APNs); Sentry (crash reports). No payment processor, advertising network or third-party analytics SDK is used.

5. Regions: MYEZ is offered on six App Store storefronts: Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates. The features are identical in all six. Content is centred on Bahrain: typed and photo comparisons search Bahrain prices and show them in Bahraini Dinar (BHD) whatever country the user picks; an international-only price is converted to BHD and labelled. Link mode uses the linked page's price.

6. Regulation and third-party material: MYEZ is not in a regulated industry. It has no sales, payments, marketplace or affiliate links; its Terms say it is not medical, financial or professional advice. No licence document applies: MYEZ hosts, streams and sells no third-party media and has no content partnerships. It shows product names only to identify products, retailer prices, AI-compiled specs, a review-praise summary (no quotes) and ratings only when a source publishes one. Images load from the publishing site or a search engine's image server. Owners may ask support@getmyez.com for removal.
