PURPOSE: a free shopping decision aid, in English and Arabic, for shoppers aged 13+ in Bahrain and the Gulf. The user types, photographs or links two products; MYEZ gathers public reviews and retailer prices; AI compiles specs and writes one comparison. The screen recording is attached to our App Review reply.

SIGN-IN: required (each comparison runs paid AI and search requests, so the allowance is per account); it also holds History and the saved preferences each verdict uses. Review account: in the Sign-In Information fields. It has finished the questionnaire; we raised its daily allowance for review (MYEZ sells nothing; no user can buy a higher limit).

NEW ACCOUNT (to test sign-up and deletion): Sign Up, any email, a 10+ character password (upper case, lower case, a digit) in Password and Confirm Password, tick "I am 13 or older and I agree to the Terms and Privacy", Create Account (Invite code is optional). No email confirmation needed. A 16-screen first-run questionnaire follows (required: language, country, 1-3 priorities, budget, brand preference, how you heard of us; age is optional; last screen: "Maybe later"). Choosing Arabic reloads the app once for right-to-left. New accounts: 3 comparisons a day, 10 a month. The header pill ("x/3 free") counts only 3 starter comparisons (fewer on a device that had MYEZ accounts); tap it for today's allowance.

HOW TO TEST:
1. MYEZ tab: Scan (default), Link, Type. Tap Type, enter "iPhone 15" and "Samsung Galaxy S24", tap Compare.
2. The first comparison or photo use shows a one-time sheet "Your comparison is written with AI" naming OpenAI and what is sent; "Agree and continue" proceeds, "Not now" cancels.
3. A comparison usually takes 20-35 s. Results: the winner, the reasons, "Where the runner-up wins" if any, each price if found (else "No confirmed price yet."). Optional "Tell us about you" or notification sheets close with "Skip for now" or "Not now".
4. Photos: Scan > "Turn on camera" (allow access) > "Open camera", photograph two products, tap Compare. Without camera access, "Or pick from gallery" picks two photos.
5. Link: two product page links from stores.
6. History: past comparisons. Log out: Profile > Log Out > Log Out.
DELETE ACCOUNT: Profile tab > gear icon (top right) > Delete account (under Account actions) > Delete account. The app returns to the sign-in screen.

REGIONS: six storefronts (Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE), identical features. Typed and photo comparisons search Bahrain prices, shown in BHD whatever country the user picks; an international-only price is converted to BHD and labelled. Link uses the linked page's price.

AI PROCESSING: product names, links (with page text), photos, and the preferences, country, language and area named on the sheet go to OpenAI to write the comparison; OpenAI may use inputs and outputs to evaluate and train models, as the sheet says. Name, email and account details are not sent.

SERVICES: OpenAI; Supabase (database, sign-in); Sign in with Apple; Google Sign-In; Railway; Upstash; Serper; retailer sites, Firecrawl, Scrape.do; Expo (APNs push); Sentry. No payment, ad or analytics SDK.

REGULATION: not regulated; no sales, payments, marketplace or affiliate links; Terms: not medical, health or financial advice. No licence applies: no hosted, streamed or sold third-party media, no content partnerships; names only identify products; review praise is summarised (no quotes); ratings only if published. Images: the store page.

No purchases, subscriptions, ads, tracking, feed, comments, chat or public profiles, so no reporting or blocking flow. SHARE: a new result shares a text summary. A comparison opened from History opens an invite sheet: a link to it (the user can hide name, result, reasons) and an invite code. REFERRALS: when an invited friend signs up and runs a first comparison, the inviter gets 5 bonus comparisons for 7 days, max 3 friends per device; no cash value.
