PURPOSE: MYEZ is a free shopping decision aid, in English and Arabic, for shoppers aged 13+ in Bahrain and the Gulf. The user types, photographs or links two products; MYEZ gathers public reviews and retailer prices; AI compiles specs and writes one comparison. The screen recording is attached to our 1.0.0 App Review reply.

SIGN-IN: required (each comparison runs paid AI and search requests, so the allowance is per account); it also holds History and preferences. Review account: the user name and password are in the Sign-In Information fields of App Review Information. It has completed the first-run questionnaire and has a raised daily allowance.

NEW ACCOUNT (to test sign-up and deletion; keep the review account): Sign Up, any email, a password of 10+ characters (upper case, lower case, a digit) in Password and Confirm Password, tick "I am 13 or older and I agree to the Terms and Privacy", Create Account. No email confirmation needed. A 16-screen first-run questionnaire follows (required: language, country, 1-3 priorities, budget, brand preference, how you heard of us; last screen: "Maybe later"). New accounts: 3 comparisons a day, 10 a month. The header pill ("x/3 free") counts only 3 starter comparisons (fewer on a device that had MYEZ accounts); tap it for today's allowance.

HOW TO TEST:
1. MYEZ tab: Scan (default), Link, Type. Tap Type, enter "iPhone 15" and "Samsung Galaxy S24", tap Compare.
2. The first comparison, camera or photo-picker use shows a one-time sheet "Your comparison is written with AI" naming OpenAI and what is sent; "Agree and continue" proceeds, "Not now" cancels.
3. A comparison usually takes 20-35 s. Results: the winner, the reasons, "Where the runner-up wins" if any, each price if found (else "No confirmed price yet."). Optional "Tell us about you" or notification sheets close with "Skip for now" or "Not now".
4. Photos: Scan > "Turn on camera" (allow access) > "Open camera", photograph two products, tap Compare. Without camera access, "Or pick from gallery" picks two photos.
5. Link: two product page links from stores.
6. History: past comparisons. Log out: Profile > Log Out > Log Out.
DELETE ACCOUNT: Profile tab > gear icon (top right) > Delete account (under Account actions) > Delete account. The app returns to the sign-in screen.

REGIONS: offered on six App Store storefronts (Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates); features are identical in all six. Content is centred on Bahrain: typed and photo comparisons search Bahrain prices, shown in BHD for all users whatever country they pick; a price found only internationally is converted to BHD and labelled. Link uses the linked page's price.

AI PROCESSING: product names, links (with page text), photos, and the profile preferences, country, language and area named on the sheet are sent to OpenAI to write the comparison. OpenAI may use inputs and outputs to evaluate and train models, as the sheet says. Name, email and account details are not sent.

SERVICES: OpenAI API (AI); Supabase (database, sign-in); Sign in with Apple; Google Sign-In; Railway (hosting); Upstash (cache); Serper (search); retailer websites and store search, with Firecrawl and Scrape.do as fallbacks; Expo (updates, push via APNs); Sentry (crash reports). No payment, ad or third-party analytics SDK.

REGULATION: not regulated; no sales, payments, marketplace or affiliate links; the Terms state it is not medical, health or financial advice. It shows names, prices, AI-compiled specs, a review-praise summary (no quotes) and ratings only when a source publishes one. Images load from the publishing sites or a search engine's image server.

No in-app purchases, subscriptions, ads or tracking. No feed, comments, chat or public profiles; Share only passes a private link to the user's own comparison (an invite can show a display name), so no reporting or blocking flow. The daily-limit screen only explains when comparisons refresh.
