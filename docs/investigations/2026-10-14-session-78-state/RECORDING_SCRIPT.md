# MYEZ review recording: owner script (aim for 3-5 minutes of video)

## A. Device prep (the day before, or at least 1 hour before)
1. **iOS**: Settings > General > Software Update. Install the latest iOS. Apple requires "the latest operating system".
2. **Build**: in the TestFlight app, install MYEZ **1.0.0 (2)**, the submitted build. Check the build number on the TestFlight app page. If TestFlight will not install 1.0.0 (2) (for example "a developer has to invite you"), stop and tell Claude. The reply says the recording uses build 1.0.0 (2) from TestFlight, so it must not be sent until that is true.
3. **Fresh install**: if MYEZ has ever been used on this phone with review account 2, delete the app and reinstall it from TestFlight. The AI consent sheet is remembered per account on each device; a reinstall clears that. Your sign-in can survive a reinstall (it is kept in the iOS Keychain). After any reinstall, open MYEZ once. If it opens signed in, go to Profile > Log Out > Log Out.
4. **Screen Recording in Control Center**: Settings > Control Center > add Screen Recording.
5. **Do Not Disturb**: turn on a Focus (Do Not Disturb) so no notification banners show. Also set Settings > Notifications > Show Previews to Never as a backup.
6. **Phone language English**, region any. Battery above 50 %. Wi-Fi on.
7. **Keyboard character preview off** (Settings > General > Keyboard > Character Preview). Otherwise each password letter pops up large on screen. Better still, copy the password from Notes and paste it into the field.
8. **Accounts**:
   - Use **review account 2** for the recording, never account 1 (account 1 is the one Apple signs in with).
   - Account 2 must already have finished the questionnaire. It must NEVER have run a comparison on this phone before you record.
   - Have a **throwaway email** ready for the Sign Up shot (an address you control, never used in MYEZ before).
9. **Dry run with YOUR OWN main account, not account 1 or 2**:
   - Run the exact typed pair you will record. Suggested: "iPhone 15" vs "Samsung Galaxy S24", the only one of six warm-up pairs that came back complete on 2026-10-11; fragrance and skincare pairs mostly ended partial at the 30 s cap. The run warms the cache. Check that the result shows prices, Pros & Cons and Specs. If it comes back thin ("No confirmed price yet." or a missing write-up), try another pair until one renders fully.
   - Run one **photo** comparison with two real products you have at home. If it fails or looks wrong, record Link mode instead (step 7b) and tell Claude, so the reply's item 1 says "a comparison of two product links" instead of "a photo comparison".
   - Copy two product page links from a **Bahrain store that prices in BHD** into Notes, in case you need Link mode. Link mode shows the price from the linked page in that page's own currency, so check in the dry run that both show BHD.
   - Sign out of your own account (Profile > Log Out > Log Out) and go back to the sign-in screen. Then remove MYEZ from the app switcher, reopen it once, and confirm it opens on "Welcome back." Remove it from the app switcher again.
10. **Google sign-in check (before the reply is sent)**: on this TestFlight build, tick the 13+ box on the sign-in screen and tap "Continue with Google" once with your own Google account. If it does not sign you in, stop and tell Claude: Apple reviewers usually tap every sign-in button, and a failure is a rejection. If it works, log out again (step 9, last bullet).

## B. Shot list (press record at the Home Screen, before launching)
1. **0:00 Launch**: open Control Center > Screen Recording, wait for the 3-second countdown, close Control Center. Tap the **MYEZ** icon. Let the splash play. The sign-in screen "Welcome back." appears.
2. **Sign in**: tap Email, type review account 2's email. Tap Password and paste the password (dots only). Tap **Sign In**. The app lands on the MYEZ tab, with Scan selected.
3. **Typed comparison**: tap **Type**. Enter the dry-run pair in Product A and Product B. Tap **Compare**.
4. **Consent sheet**: "Your comparison is written with AI" appears. Hold 2-3 seconds so the text is readable, scroll it slowly if it scrolls, then tap **Agree and continue**.
5. **Loading**: wait on "Finding products..." (usually 20-40 s). Do NOT tap Cancel.
6. **Results**: scroll slowly through the Top match badge, the prices, "Why this fits you", "Where the runner-up wins", "What we know" and "Dig deeper". Tap **Pros & Cons**, then **Specs**, to expand them. Tap the back arrow.
7. **Photo comparison**: tap **Scan** > **Turn on camera** (or "Open camera") > **Allow** in the iOS prompt. Photograph product 1, then product 2, then tap **Compare**. Wait on "Identifying your products", show the result briefly, tap back.
   - 7b (only if the photo dry run failed): tap **Link**, paste the two BHD product links, tap **Compare**, show the result, tap back.
8. **History**: tap the **History** tab, show the comparisons you just ran, tap one to reopen it, then go back.
9. **Log out**: tap the **Profile** tab, scroll to "Danger zone", tap **Log Out**, then **Log Out** in the alert. The sign-in screen appears.
10. **Sign up**: tap **Sign Up** ("New here? Sign Up"). Enter the throwaway email, a password that meets the rule (10+ characters with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter and a digit), and the same password in Confirm Password. Tick "I am 13 or older and I agree to the Terms and Privacy". Tap **Create Account**.
11. **Questionnaire** (16 screens, move at a steady pace):
    1. Continue.
    2. **English** + Next. (Never pick Arabic: it reloads the app mid-recording.)
    3. Continue.
    4. **Bahrain** (area optional) + Next.
    5. I'm in.
    6. An age + Next, or Prefer not to say.
    7. Prefer not to say.
    8. 1-3 priorities + Next.
    9. A budget (pick **Mid-range**) + Next.
    10. A brand choice + Next.
    11. A "Where did you hear about us?" answer + Next.
    12. Continue.
    13. Wait for the checklist, then Continue.
    14. Wait on the loading screen.
    15. Compare your first product (this only moves to the next screen; it does not start a comparison).
    16. **Maybe later**.

    The app lands on the MYEZ tab.
12. **Do NOT run a comparison on this new account.** Do not tap any "Popular comparisons" row or "Today's tailored pick": one tap starts a comparison.
13. **Delete account**: tap the **Profile** tab > **gear icon** (top right). Edit Profile opens. Scroll to "Account actions" and tap **Delete account**. In the alert "Are you sure? This cannot be undone.", tap **Delete account**. The sign-in screen appears. Hold for 2 seconds.
14. Stop the recording: tap the red status indicator > Stop.

## C. Do NOT show
- Any password in clear text (dots only; key previews off).
- "Continue with Google" (never tap it in the recording), "Forgot Password?", the Share button, or the "x/3 free" counter pill in the header (it reads "0/3 free" on raised accounts and opens the limit screen).
- Personal notifications, messages, other apps, your photo library contents beyond the two product photos, or your own main account.

## D. Trim and export
1. Photos app > the recording > Edit. Trim only the dead time before the MYEZ icon tap and after the sign-in screen at the end. The video must still start with the app launch, so do not cut that out. Do not speed it up or add text. Tap Done.
2. Watch it once fully: check for any visible password, notification or personal data.
3. Move it to the computer you will use for App Store Connect (AirDrop, or Share > Save to Files > iCloud Drive). It stays a .mov or .mp4 file.
4. In App Store Connect > the app > App Review (Resolution Center) message, attach the video to the reply. Apple's attachment size limit was not checked. If the upload is refused, re-export at a lower resolution from Photos (Share > Options) or ask Claude for help.
5. Before sending: if the Bright Data token has been renewed since 2026-10-11, tell Claude, so "Bright Data (search fallback)" is added to the services list in the reply and the notes.
