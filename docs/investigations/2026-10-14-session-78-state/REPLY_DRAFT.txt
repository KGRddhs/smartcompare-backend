Hello App Review team,

Thank you for reviewing MYEZ. Our answers follow in your order; the same information is in the App Review Information Notes field.

1. Screen recording: attached. It was recorded on an iPhone running the latest iOS, with the submitted build 1.0.0 (2) from TestFlight. It begins at app launch and shows: signing in with an email account; a typed comparison with the one-time AI consent sheet and the result; a photo comparison; the History tab; logging out; registering a new account with Sign Up and its first-run questionnaire; and deleting that account in the app, which returns to the sign-in screen. MYEZ has no user-generated content shared between users and no paid content or features, so there is no reporting, blocking or purchase flow.

2. Purpose and audience: MYEZ is a free shopping decision aid, in English and Arabic, for shoppers aged 13 and over in Bahrain and the Gulf. It replaces the many separate searches needed to compare two products' specs, reviews and local prices. The user types, photographs or links two products; MYEZ gathers public specs, review snippets and retailer prices and returns one AI-written comparison: the recommended product, the reasons, where the other product wins, and each product's price when one is found.

3. Setup and access: the app opens on the sign-in screen. Please sign in with the user name and password in the Sign-In Information fields of App Review Information. It has completed the first-run questionnaire and has a raised daily limit. To test registration and deletion, please create a new account so the review account stays usable: tap Sign Up, enter any email address and a password of 10 or more characters with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter and a digit, tick "I am 13 or older and I agree to the Terms and Privacy" and tap Create Account. No email confirmation is needed. To compare: MYEZ tab > Type > two products (e.g. "iPhone 15" and "Samsung Galaxy S24") > Compare > "Agree and continue" on the one-time sheet. A comparison usually takes 20 to 40 seconds. Delete account: Profile tab > gear icon (top right) > Edit Profile > Account actions > Delete account > Delete account.

4. External services: OpenAI API (identifies products from names, links and photos; extracts specs, prices and review summaries; writes the comparison; moderates output); Supabase (database, email sign-in); Sign in with Apple; Google Sign-In; Railway (hosting); Upstash (cache, usage counters); Serper (web and shopping search); public retailer websites and store search services, fetched by our servers, with Firecrawl and Scrape.do as fallbacks; Expo (app updates, notifications via APNs); Sentry (crash reports). No payment processor, advertising network or third-party analytics SDK is used.

5. Regions: the features are the same in every region and there is no geographic restriction. Content is centred on Bahrain: typed and photo comparisons search prices for Bahrain and show them in Bahraini Dinar (BHD) for all users; a price found only on an international listing is converted to BHD and labelled as converted. A Link comparison uses the price on the linked page. English and Arabic (right to left) work everywhere.

6. Regulation and third-party material: MYEZ does not operate in a regulated industry. It has no sales, payments, marketplace or affiliate links; its Terms state that it is not medical, health, financial or professional advice. It shows publicly available product information: names, prices, short review excerpts with their source name, and star ratings only when a source publishes one (never AI-generated). Product images load directly from the websites that publish them or a search engine's image server; we do not host or resell them. Brand names only identify products.
