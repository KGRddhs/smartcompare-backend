ROOT CAUSE: The compare does not fit in the 30 s server cap, and the app aborts at 35 s (SmartCompareApp/src/services/api.ts:48 COMPARE_TIMEOUT_MS). Each product runs in series: category LLM 0.7-2 s, then the unified Serper search (12 s bound, 5 timeouts in the log), then Phase 1 (reviews 10 s, specs 8 s, and a 15 s price race that missed 23 of 30 times), then the refills. Phase 2 (5 s) is skipped on 20 of 30 products. Tier 2 (4 s) timed out 6 of 6 times and filled nothing in the captures, and Tier 3 (3 s) follows it. Neither tier checks the time left. Pros come only from the verdict, which needs about 6 s once the pair returns (iPhone: Phase-1 end to moderation took 6.1 s). With under about 4.5 s left, the cap wins and the partial has no pros. Serper legs take 6-13 s, and the loop still stalls for 4 s or more: product 1's reviews leg started 4 s after its search returned, and the cap returned at 34.2 s. A second run is not faster, because partials write no cache and specs or reviews built after a search timeout are never cached (FY19). Only the already-cached pairs pass, at 26-29 s.
