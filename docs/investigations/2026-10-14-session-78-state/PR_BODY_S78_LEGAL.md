## S78-LEGAL: personal data out of the legal texts, recipients by category, Terms of Service v2

The owner asked, on 2026-10-14, for four things across every published legal surface:
- remove any data about the team: the home address first, then personal data, secrets and backend detail;
- list third-party services only where needed;
- make the Terms of Service strong and compliant with Apple's rules.

**Owner rulings.** Address: interim now, a P.O. Box later. Name: kept, with the Apple spelling. Vendors: by category. Date: bumped server-side now.

**Where the rulings live** (session-78 state folder): `specs/RULINGS_S78_LEGAL.md` (LR1-LR11), `RULINGS_S78_LEGAL_SPEC_REVIEW.md` (LT1-LT11, M1-M10) and `RED_GATE_S78_LEGAL.md` (G1-G12).

All text was generated through the U8 machinery (snapshot templates, then `fill_in_legal.py`, then `render_legal_landing.py`). Nothing was hand-edited.

### What changes for users

**Controller identity**
- It reads: "Husain Aseeri, an individual app developer in the Kingdom of Bahrain, trading as MYEZ".
- The postal line reads: "Kingdom of Bahrain (our postal address is available on request at privacy@getmyez.com)".
- The home address is gone from all six legal markdown files, all four API payloads (EN and AR) and the six landing legal pages.
- The later P.O. Box swap is a data change (the fill-in value) plus a re-measure of the page-address count pins.
- Bahrain PDPL Art. 17(1)(a) requires the full name, the field of activity and an address, but not a home address. The interim "on request" line is a known Art. 17 gap until the P.O. Box exists.

**Privacy s5 (recipients)**
- Recipients are now listed by category.
- Only OpenAI (Apple 5.1.2(i)), Apple and Google sign-in, and the retailer websites stay named.
- New sentence: "We will tell you the names of our service providers on request".
- s7 (transfers) lists countries only.

**Terms v2** (19 sections):
- **Apple section:** Exhibit B items 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9 and 10, with Standard-EULA precedence: the licence stays Apple's Standard EULA, and these Terms cover the service.
- **Liability:** it opens with the carve-outs Bahraini law requires (fraud, gross negligence, wilful misconduct, death or personal injury).
- **Indemnity:** a narrow user indemnity (s17).
- **Minors:** users under 18 need a parent's or guardian's permission.
- **Acceptable use:** stronger.
- **AI and accuracy:** the AI, price-accuracy and no-advice disclaimers are fuller.
- **Changes:** material changes are announced when they are published.

**Effective date**
- 2026-10-14 in all twelve anchors. `legal_routes` now derives its date from `consent_service.TERMS_VERSION`.
- `consent.ts` reaches phones only with the first post-approval OTA. Until then, phones record terms_version 2026-10-11 (LR4 / A4).

**Elsewhere**
- The crawler User-Agent contact is now `support@getmyez.com`; it was a personal address sent to third-party sites.
- The `pyproject` authors entries now show the role address.
- A personal-looking value in a test fixture was replaced.
