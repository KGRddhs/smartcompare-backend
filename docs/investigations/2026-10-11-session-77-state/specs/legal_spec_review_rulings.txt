== wording_issues_en_ar
- P2/T8 EN 'We do not sell your personal data to data brokers or advertisers, and we do not share it with them' narrows an absolute promise ('We do not sell your personal data' / 'your personal data is never sold') to two buyer classes. A reader may infer that sale to anyone else is allowed. The documents permit no such sale anywhere else, because the d3c (k) scan forbids it. This follows the owner-approved Option B starting point and avoids labelling the OpenAI exchange a 'sale', which is counsel item 8.2. Ruling R10 accepts it as a recorded limit and offers an alternative.
- P5C EN 'our mailbox provider, Google (Gmail)' / AR 'مزوّد صندوق بريدنا' is a simplification (spec L4 is accurate). The inbox is the operator's personal Gmail, not one owned by the named controller. Acceptable only with L4 recorded and the controller-owned mailbox follow-up.
- P5G AR 'إذا استخدمت تسجيل الدخول عبر Google، تشارك Google معنا ...' is grammatical MSA (a present-tense jawab after إذا). The more formal 'فإن Google تشارك معنا' is optional polish and not required.
- /bot EN 'If you run a store and want us to stop or slow down, email ...' implies an ability to stop or slow price look-ups per store. That is acceptable as an invitation (not a time-bound promise; Q3 correctly excludes the 5-day term). The page is silent on the runtime look-ups' User-Agent and robots behaviour (L5); it does not over-claim.
- Verified faithful, consistent with the existing AR terms: P2, P4 (both sentences), P5G, P5C, T8 tail, T4, the L2 lede and og text, and the /bot AR paragraph. The entity line decodes byte-equal to the spec block (246 chars, upper-case 4-hex). The P2/T8 AR reuse 'برنامج مشاركة البيانات لدى OpenAI', 'المدخلات التي نرسلها إليها والمخرجات التي تنتجها منها' and 'تقييم نماذجها وتدريبها' exactly as variant C does, and P5C's 'وفق شروط Google الخاصة' matches line 87's 'وفق شروطها الخاصة'.
- Accuracy vs code: T4 matches usage_service.py. Verified: TIER_LIMITS :138 (lifetime 3 / daily 3 / monthly 10), the lifetime-free path :360 bumping both counters via _bump_counters :314, and monthly_cap = monthly + bonus :356. The P2/P4 exchange wording matches the D3 record: OpenAI org enrolled for complimentary daily tokens (Build tier, positive balance required); 'some free use' does not over-claim. The /bot runtime split is correct: QarenBot/NAMED_AGENT appears only in robots_eval.py, sitemap_discovery_service.py, search_descriptor_service.py and scripts/resolve_search_descriptors.py, and that script imports search_descriptor_service.
