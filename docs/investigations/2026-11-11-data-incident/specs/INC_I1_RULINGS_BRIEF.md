I1-1 SCOPE. The text-compare picture: the cascade tiers 1.5b, 1 and 3, and the noon adapter's price dict. Unchanged: the camera path, the KB card image, the price-page override's own policy (ENABLE_IMAGE_PIPELINE_V2), INC-X2's time budget, the client.

I1-2 FLAG. One new dark flag ENABLE_IMAGE_RELEVANCE_GUARD, read per call with the house idiom, default OFF; flag OFF byte-identical on every entry (pinned). Knob IMAGE_RELEVANCE_CANDIDATES (default 5, clamped 1..10; garbage falls back to the default).

I1-3 THE RELEVANCE TEST (deterministic, pure Python, no new dependency). A candidate image is accepted only when the text that came with it (Serper Images: title, source and the link's host; knowledge graph: its title; organic: the result title and link host) carries (a) a brand token of the product, from the title or as the source / host (e.g. dior.com for Dior), when the product's brand is non-empty, AND (b) at least one significant token of the product name (the line; stop words and concentration words excluded), case-folded and accent-stripped, with the brand alias folding the repository already has (the spec names it). When the brand is empty, (b) plus a fragrance word (perfume, parfum, eau, edt, edp, cologne, fragrance, and the Arabic word for perfume) is required instead of (a). So "The Tyger" by William Blake fails (no fragrance word), and a Bvlgari Tygar bottle titled with "Bvlgari" or "Bulgari" and "Tygar" passes.

I1-4 THE QUERY. Under the flag, Tier 1 asks Serper Images for "<brand> <name> <concentration> perfume" (the concentration only when known; the word perfume only for a fragrance-category product) with IMAGE_RELEVANCE_CANDIDATES results, walks them in order and takes the first that passes I1-3 and I1-5; none passes = the tier misses (the next tier or the placeholder). The spec measures whether Serper Images bills per query or per result and whether the image daily budget counter (try_consume_serper_image_credit) is per call.

I1-5 SCHEME. Under the flag only https URLs are accepted on every tier (an http candidate is skipped, logged once).

I1-6 TIERS 1.5b AND 3. Tier 1.5b applies I1-3 to each knowledge-graph / organic / images item before taking it; Tier 3's picked URL must belong to an organic result whose title passes I1-3.
