# The perfume result page: what you will see, and what I need from you

This is a design proposal. Nothing in it is built or switched on, and nothing here is fixed in the live app yet.

## 1. The coming days: no AI text

You ruled "wait for Claude". So until Claude writes text, every comparison has no written explanation, no pros and cons, no review highlights and, in practice, no perfume details. Prices, photos, star ratings and the short fact lines do not need AI and show whatever the search found. Section by section:

| Section | The apps you have today | The new page (same days) |
|---|---|---|
| Photos | the photo, or a grey tile with no words | the photo, or "No photo found yet" |
| Details under the name | empty | "100ml · EDP", or "Size not listed yet" |
| Price | the price, or "No confirmed price yet." (a price for another bottle size is hidden) | each price with its bottle size and a price per ml; a price for another bottle size is shown with its size and "Different bottle sizes, so we do not compare the prices." |
| Why this fits you | a grey note, then two English sentences, even on the Arabic app | "The written explanation is unavailable this time. The facts below are what we could confirm.", then the two sentences in Arabic |
| Key differences | not shown | price, price per ml, size and concentration, where known |
| Score bars | 0 to 4 bars, some of them fake | the same five rows every time; an unknown row says "Limited data" |
| What we know | the price pill disappears | three pills; a missing price says "Not confirmed" |
| Reviews | "Reviews are still coming in." | the rating or "No rating available", and "Review highlights are unavailable this time." |
| Pros and cons | the whole row disappears | always there, with one honest line |
| Specs | just the two names | the fixed list of perfume details, or "Not listed by the stores we can read yet" |
| Feedback card | unchanged | unchanged |

The phone mock shows this as its first screen, on Aventus vs Layton.

## 2. What stays the same on the apps people have today

Both apps people have today keep their layout until they take a later update with the new page. Most server changes are invisible to them. Three are visible, so each has its own question:
- the honest bars: fake bars stop appearing, and a bar can no longer be won by a missing value (questions 1 to 4; the overall winner does not move, see question 5);
- the details line under the names (question 10);
- later, Arabic sentences for Arabic users (question 13).
The price for another bottle size is NOT shown on today's apps; they keep "No confirmed price yet." and make no cheaper claim.

## 3. The order the pieces ship in

1. Honest bars first: the only change you can see on today's apps without an update.
2. Arabic codes and key differences next: invisible until the new page ships.
3. The new page last: after you approve this design and read every new Arabic line, in the update AFTER Friday's second update.
Every switch stays paused while the AI is down, one switch per test window, each on your yes. Checks that need AI text (the explanation, pros and cons, review highlights, perfume details) wait for the Claude switch; everything else can be checked before it.
