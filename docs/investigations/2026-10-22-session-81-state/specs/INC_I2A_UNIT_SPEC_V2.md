## 9. Stated limits
- Tier 1.5b after the paid Tier 1: one credit where the payload alone would do.
- Up to 3 s of tail wall, only with no override and the search still running after the verdict.
- A picture landing after the bind shows from the NEXT compare (cache), never pushed.
- The fallback picture can be another bottle size of the same product (R-3); ON, the shown picture follows the shown listing, else the cascade, else the Phase-1 listing.
- A wrong but accepted picture stays up to 7 days or until the guard version moves; a cached hotlinked URL can die.
- R OFF and R ON name the same product differently (R-12): one paid miss after the resolver flip.
- INC-I2b (and any GUARD_SET edit) must bump PICK_GUARD_RULES (R-19); old entries expire by TTL.
- Jobs outlive the request by at most 20 s (clamp 30); shutdown's DRAIN_TIMEOUT (8 s, app/main.py:474) may abandon one.
- The `specs` event, source_trace and DEBUG timings show the image as at Phase 1 end; the drain's bind line carries wait_ms=0.
- `serper_await` measures T2-08 only on the ON path.
