## Addendum 2026-10-10 ~22:50: UG12 (owner request: "mention that we collect data and that they should consent that at some point we own or sell the data if this is good and legal")

Assessment (orchestrator): collection + consent is already stated (the consent sheet, privacy sections 2-4, App Privacy rows). OWNERSHIP of users' personal data is not a thing the law grants (PDPL data subjects keep their rights); ownership of DERIVED data is. SELLING identifiable personal data would (a) make the app "tracking" under Apple's definition (sharing with a data broker: ATT prompt, NSPrivacyTracking true, App Privacy Tracking = Yes), (b) contradict the consent sheet, the description ("we don't sell your data") and privacy section 4, (c) need separate explicit consent under the PDPL and counsel review, (d) raise the App Review risk under 5.1.2. NOT for 1.0.
