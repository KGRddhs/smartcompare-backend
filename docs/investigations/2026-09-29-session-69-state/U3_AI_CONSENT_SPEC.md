## 2. Target

R1 **One-time AI-processing consent sheet** before the FIRST compare or camera scan (whichever comes first), shown after login/onboarding, not inside onboarding: title "Your comparison is generated by AI"; body names the provider ("Qaren sends the product names or photos you enter to OpenAI to identify the products and write the comparison. Your name, email and account are never sent. You can read how we handle data in the Privacy Policy.") with a link to the in-app legal screen; two actions: "Agree and continue" (persists `ai_processing_consent = {version, at}` the way `consent.ts` persists the Terms acceptance, and continues the pending action) and "Not now" (returns to Home; the compare is not started; the sheet appears again on the next attempt). No compare or scan is dispatched until consent exists. EN + native MSA AR; copy policy applies.
R2 **Camera pre-sheet**: the first time the camera or photo picker is opened, the same consent (if not yet given) precedes the permission prompt; a granted consent is never re-asked (persisted with a version; bump the version only when the disclosure text changes materially).
R3 **Server record**: the consent version and timestamp ride the existing consent endpoint/columns if `consent_service.py` has a generic slot (spec-review decides); otherwise the client persists locally and the backend change is a follow-up issue — the guideline requires permission, not a server record.
R4 **The opt-out toggle (per D3):**
  - **D3 = A (recommended):** remove the Profile "Help improve AI quality" toggle and its i18n keys; remove `ai_sharing_enabled` from the preferences UI (the column may stay); privacy policy §11 is deleted by U8. `select_client_for_user` and the `OPENAI_API_KEY_PRIVATE` branch are deleted from `openai_service.py` (dead code) with their tests updated.
  - **D3 = B:** route every one of the 17 call sites through `select_client_for_user(user_prefs)`; unset = OFF (opt-in); Ahmed provides `OPENAI_API_KEY_PRIVATE`; the toggle's default and copy match the server; a pytest pins that an opted-out user never hits the shared client.
R5 **PM-3:** remove "Your budget … never shared" from `Step05Trust` (EN, AR, the `defaultValue`), or reword to what is true ("Your budget range is used to tailor the verdict and is not shared with retailers").
R6 Nothing else: no flag; no change to what is sent to OpenAI.
