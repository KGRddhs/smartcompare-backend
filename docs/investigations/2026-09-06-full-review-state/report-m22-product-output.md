## 0 Stop the line

**0.1 — Which product wins is decided by artifacts of the rubric, not by evidence, and the payload says so out loud.**
At the prod flag state, `compute_scores` stamps **464 of 864 product_0 dimension cells (53.7 %) as missing data** on the lane's 9-category × 16-pattern grid — and it does that *to cells it measured*. Two identical, fully-specified, equally-rated, fully-fact-checked products get **4 of 6 dimensions stamped no-data** because an array tie-collapse converts "equal" into "absent" (`PO-RUBRIC-02`, `scoring_service.py:2113-2155`). The one dimension that then decides the winner is *also* stamped missing: a 4 % price gap on otherwise identical electronics produces `overall [72.0, 58.0]`, `win_margin 14.0`, with `value_score` supplying all 14.0 points while appearing in both products' `missing_data` and rendering `N/A` in `dimension_winners` (`PO-RUBRIC-01`). The text handed to the paid gpt-4o verdict prompt therefore reads `Score winner: Brand A (clear lead)` and `Dimension leaders: performance=N/A, value=N/A, build quality=N/A …` **in the same block** — the model is ordered to justify a lead it is simultaneously told nothing supports. For `fashion` and `other` — the fallback bucket for every query the classifier cannot place — there are no numeric spec fields at all, so `_score_specs` returns exactly `1.0` for every product and 35–40 % of the score is deleted before the comparison starts (`PO-RUBRIC-03`). And a product with **no data at all beats a product measured and found mediocre in 9 of 9 categories** (`PO-RUBRIC-09`): an unrated product outscores a 2.0-star one by 2.5–4.0 points because `MISSING_SCORE = 50` sits above `_normalize_review(2.0) = 40`.

**0.2 — Two deterministic pre-LLM gates are wrong, and they are the only part of the product that is user-reachable at this instant.**
Both vendors are dead, so nothing else runs — but the L1 content-safety prefilter and the deterministic category classifier fire before any API call. `content_blocklist.json:53` still carries the bare Arabic token **أفيون** in `illegal_drugs`, while the English side was tightened to multi-word forms in `9f6e498` — a commit whose own message says it was fixing "L1 query prefilter rejecting YSL Black Opium", and which shipped five EN regression tests and no Arabic ones. Pharmacy is a stated GCC launch category.

**0.3 — The price rung that does most of the work is armed to ship a 1000× error labelled "genuine Bahrain price", and the metric that would catch it is itself inflated.**
`detect_currency` consults `CURRENCY_SYMBOLS` (`{$,£,€,¥}`) and an 11-code `CURRENCY_CODES`, and never `GCC_CURRENCY_SYMBOLS` — which is where `"BD" → BHD` lives. The same rung stamps `TRY / PLN / CHF / SEK / DKK / CAD / EGP / JOD` amounts as unconverted BHD (`PO-PRICE-TRUTH-02`), and the four GCC neighbour glyphs (`1,399 د.إ → 1399 BHD`) still ship because `ENABLE_SHOPPING_STRICT_CURRENCY` has never been set on Railway (`PO-PRICE-TRUTH-03`, status of M13-09). Meanwhile the `local_bhd` label — the strongest member of `_GENUINE_BH_SOURCE_METHODS`, pinned 1:1 to the genuine-BH-share KPI — is decided **only** by "we did not convert", never by retailer country: **1,214 of 1,484 `local_bhd` cache rows (81.8 %) are not on a Bahrain URL, and 857 are a `google.com` Shopping search link** (`PO-RECORDED-MEASURED-02`, measured over all 3,945 `product_prices` rows). **0.4 — The honesty surface is the least honest surface.**
On the warm-cache shape that is normal production, `verify_spec_citations` grades every field `unverified`, and `compute_confidence` then reads `citation_count = verified + likely + unverified + flagged` and fires `specs_strong` on `count >= 8`: the pill reads **"High" at 0 % verified**, beside a price pill that reads "strong" next to its own `price_verified=False` at 89.8 % deviation (`PO-FACTCHECK-CONFIDENCE-03`; 34 of 46 recorded product sides have `specs_verified == 0`). `verify_price` itself is **systematically inverted** at the prod default: fed a 250.0 figure mis-stamped BHD (the exact 9.8× error class the currency programme exists to kill) against AED-250 shopping rows it returns `price_verified: True, deviation 0.0 %`, and fed the *correct* 25.5 BHD it returns `False` at 89.8 % (`PO-FACTCHECK-CONFIDENCE-04` — the fix is written and the flag is off). `_score_reliability` returns exactly `0.3` for any all-unverified fact-check regardless of size, so one named dimension in **every** category is a dead per-product constant moved only by a `price_verified=True` that is itself fabricated when there is nothing to check (`PO-FACTCHECK-CONFIDENCE-01`). ### 1d — The `#101` product call: `win_margin 0.0`

**This is Ahmed's decision. The lane's job was to put the numbers on the table, and here they are.** All figures are measured through the real `compute_scores` over a 9-category × 16-pattern × 7-flag-state × 2-order grid (2,016 runs).

**The problem `#101` exists to fix.** `MISSING_SCORE = 50` sits *above* a measured-bad signal: `_normalize_review(2.0 stars) = 40`. At the prod flag state, a product with **no rating at all beats a 2.0-star product in 9 of 9 categories** (electronics 55.0 vs 52.0; makeup/haircare/fragrances 52.5 vs 48.5; grocery/other 53.8 vs 50.2), and the winner's `missing_data` lists **all six** dimensions while the loser's lists four. A product the pipeline failed to gather data for outranks one it measured and found mediocre. That is live today and it is what `#101` targets.

**What `ENABLE_MISSING_DIM_RENORM` actually does when flipped.**

| shape | flag OFF (prod) | flag ON |
|---|---|---|
| unrated vs 2.0-star | void wins **9/9** | inversion fixed — but the pair becomes an **exact 0.0 tie in 9/9** |
| total data void vs measured-bad | void wins **6/9** | void still wins **8/9** (verifier's construction; the finder measured 4/9) — `effective_weight_total` hits 0, the degenerate guard logs, and the code **falls back to the un-renormalised legacy sum** |
| `win_margin == 0.0` rate | 13/144 = **9.0 %** | 31/144 = **21.5 %** (45/144 = 31.3 % with everything on) |
| which pairs became ties | — | **18 of the 18 new zeros are exactly the rating-gap pairs** (4.9★/900 reviews vs unrated; unrated vs 2★) — the comparisons the app should be *most* confident about |

**The two facts that make this a product call rather than an engineering one.**
*First*, at an exact 0.0 tie **there is no tie-break in prod**: the coverage tie-break exists but is gated behind a dark flag, so the winner is whichever product the caller passed first (`PO-RUBRIC-05`). Turning `#101` on without that tie-break converts a wrong-but-explicable winner into a hidden coin flip on 21.5 % of comparisons.
*Second*, this is not `#101`'s problem alone: `ENABLE_SPEC_FIELD_NORM` (`#100`) does the same thing from the other side — it collapses the `5000 mAh` vs `5 Ah` artifact to 0.0 **and** ties a genuine 16 GB vs 512 GB gap at 0.0 via its ≥10× guard (`PO-RUBRIC-10`). Both flags manufacture ties. The tie-break is the shared precondition.

**The options, stated without a recommendation:**

- **(A) Flip `#101` as designed and accept 21.5 % zero-margin.** Cheapest. Buys the inversion fix; sells the ability to say "clear lead" on exactly the rating gaps the product exists to adjudicate; leaves the total-void shape broken in 8/9 and the winner order-dependent on every new tie.
- **(B) Do not renormalise — clamp the sentinel instead.** Move the missing injection from the midpoint `50` to the dampened band **floor (45)**, below `_normalize_review(2.0)=40`'s neighbourhood, so a data void can never outrank measured-bad. This is the "at minimum" half of M18 `PO-rubric-03`, is independent of the renorm, and **does not manufacture ties**. It does not fix the deeper issue that a void is scored at all.
- **(C) Renormalise, and give the degenerate path a real rule.** When `effective_weight_total <= 0` because one side is a total void, stop falling back to the legacy sum: either route that comparison to `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` (the orchestrator already has that path) or award it to the measured product outright. Closes the 8/9 residual; still needs the tie-break.
- **(D) Renormalise + order-symmetric tie-break + say the true thing.** Surface `too_close_to_call` / `decided_on_coverage` alongside `win_margin`, and let the verdict copy say "we could only separate these on data availability". Turns the 21.5 % from a hidden coin flip into an honest product statement — and is the only option that also unblocks `#100`. Most work; changes user-facing copy, so it is OTA-gated on the client half.
- **(E) Do not flip anything; fix coverage first.** Treat missing data as an extraction problem, not a scoring problem. Defensible while `PO-PROMPTS-11` is unresolved — but it leaves the 9/9 inversion live for however long that takes.

**The evidence that should decide it, and which is missing.** Nobody knows the production frequency of these shapes. The 9.0 %/21.5 %/31.3 % figures are frequencies over the lane's **constructed** grid, not over traffic; `search_logs` stores no `category_used` and the 27 recorded payloads are all ≤ 2026-06-21. The decision would be much better informed by one week of real post-restore traffic with a `win_margin` histogram. **Do not implement U10 until this is answered.**

#### The canary for the unflagged prompt work (U15), and what it costs

A prompt is text inside a Python module: there is no per-call `os.getenv` that can fork it, so **flag-OFF byte-identity is meaningless for U15 and the canary is the entire gate.** The canary that would actually validate it:

1. **Offline, before deploy** — render the real `_build_specs_prompt` / `COMPARISON_SYSTEM` with the flag in both states and diff the rendered strings; assert the fence tests above; assert `resolved_models()` is unchanged.
2. **Post-deploy, one compare at a time** — `eval_runner --subset smoke20 --concurrency 1` against the `54b603e8-…` anchor, judged on the **axis averages** (winner / specs / factual), never `pass_rate`. Watch the `[specs] no-fabrication guard dropped N uncited field(s)` log line and the spec-coverage distribution; **coverage falling is the success signal**, not a regression.
3. **Then N=20 real queries** through `/api/v1/text/compare?nocache=true`, scored on the manual rubric this lane used (winner name == winner index == prose subject; provenance labels honest; no score internals in pros/cons; cons non-empty and not a data-gap complaint), because the eval's gold set contains none of the shapes these findings are about.
4. **Watch `WINNER_INDEX_MISMATCH`** in the logs — it already exists and is the cheapest signal that a prompt edit moved the model's winner away from the deterministic one.

**The OpenAI prompt-cache re-warm cost.** The lane measured the shipped prefixes with tiktoken: the specs system prefix is **2,121 tokens** and the verdict prefix **2,241** — both above the 1,024-token caching threshold and both correctly ordered static-prefix-first. Price (555), reviews (769), parser (658), critique (293) and `extract_specs_targeted` (~41, with a dynamic value at position 0) are ineligible either way. Consequences, and the pricing half is provider documentation rather than a measurement in this campaign: **any edit to a cached prefix invalidates it**, so the first call after each deploy pays full input price for those ~2.1–2.2 k tokens and the cache re-warms on the next request; entries evict after roughly 5–10 minutes of inactivity. At today's ≈ 0 traffic every call is already a cache miss, so the re-warm cost right now is **nil**; at launch traffic it is one full-price prefix per cold window per prompt after each deploy. The real hazard is structural, not monetary: **an edit that inserts any dynamic text before the static prefix destroys cache eligibility permanently** — which is exactly the state `extract_specs_targeted` is already in. Keep every prompt edit after the static-prefix boundary, or accept that the prompt stops being cacheable at all.

## 3 What could not be known

Recorded so a later session does not mistake absence of evidence for evidence of absence.

1. **No live compare was possible.** OpenAI returns `429 insufficient_quota`; the Serper balance is −50. Every claim in this report is one of: the real runtime function imported and called offline; a recorded Supabase payload; the 414-page `_proof` corpus; or a static read at `76ace90`. **Nothing was measured end-to-end through `/compare` against prod, and no request was sent to Railway by any lane.**
2. **Everything about model behaviour is modelled, not measured.** All 22 prompt bodies were rendered through the real modules; how a 2026-09 model responds to them is unknown. Where a HEAD prompt change could have moved the input distribution — most sharply for the 83 % scrub-destruction rate and the 8 %/15 % never-empty-cons rates — the finding says so.
3. **The recorded corpus is a mid-June engine.** All 27 `comparisons` rows are `created_at ≤ 2026-06-21`, i.e. pre-M20/M21 and pre-score-forbid prompt. They are excellent evidence about the *response contract* (which was rebuilt through HEAD's real `build_comparison_response`) and weak evidence about *current model output*.
4. **The eval's four grading axes were never scored.** `data/eval_gold*.json` contains none of the 27 recorded queries, so `grade_price`/`grade_specs`/`grade_winner` would all return vacuously `True` and `grade_factual` has an empty forbidden list. Authoring gold entries would have been fabricating the truth set; the lane used the eval **extractors** plus a manual rubric instead.
5. **Production frequencies are unknown for every scoring shape.** The 9.0 %/21.5 %/31.3 % zero-margin rates, the 53.7 % missing-cell rate and the 84 % constant-badge rate are frequencies over the lane's **constructed** 2,016-run grid. `search_logs` stores no `category_used`, so the real production category mix has never been measured.
6. **The partial rate is structurally unmeasurable at `76ace90`.** Partials log `success: true` and no counter exists. The rate is inferred from p95 = 23.8 s / p99 = 30.0 s against a 30 s cap.
7. **The `local_bhd` host classification is an upper bound.** The lane's Bahrain-host test is a hostname heuristic (`.bh` / `bh.` / `bahrain.`), and `gcc.luluhypermarket.com`, `www.noon.com` and `en-bh.6thstreet.com` serve Bahrain from non-`.bh` hosts. The 857 `google.com` rows and the 89 `uae.` / `/en-sa` rows are unambiguous and are what the finding rests on.
8. **No cache round trip.** The read-only rule barred writing to Upstash or Supabase, so `#107`'s cache-roll residual is proven from the resolver's measured fallback plus the write path, not from a live key.
9. **Non-fragrance price coverage is thin.** The `_proof` corpus is 251 fragrance pages; makeup = 4, skincare = 7, electronics = 3, and haircare / supplements / grocery / fashion = **0**. Every non-fragrance page was checked individually rather than sampled, but the per-category leak rates rest on 3–7 pages.
10. **The rendered/adapter rungs were never driven.** `shopify_json`, `woo_store_api`, `salla_api`, `occ_rest_bhd`, `magento_graphql_bhd`, `rest_json_bhd`, `zyte_render_bhd`, firecrawl and scrapedo had their stamps classified but need live endpoints. `PO-PRICE-TRUTH-08`'s fan-out relabel is therefore static evidence with the corpus supplying only the input distribution.
11. **Local pip is OFF-LOCK on six pins** (fastapi 0.115.0 vs 0.141.1, starlette 0.38.6 vs 1.6.0, openai 2.21.0 vs 3.3.1, pydantic 2.7.0 vs 2.13.4, httpx 0.27.0 vs 0.28.1, supabase 2.28.0 vs 2.31.0). None of this lane's findings depends on framework introspection or SDK shape — they are plain-Python dict/tuple/list behaviour and pure-arithmetic scoring — but the `/url/compare` `AttributeError`'s rendered **body shape** could differ under the pinned FastAPI (it would still be a 500).
12. **Twenty-five P0/P1 rows never got an independent second-vote agent** (the batch died at the usage limit). They stand at their verify-pass severity, re-derived by the orchestrator against code; that is a strong vote but it is one reviewer, not two, and it is recorded as such in every trail.
13. **Arabic is untestable from recorded data.** Zero of the 27 payloads and zero of the 335 distinct logged queries contain an Arabic codepoint, and `metadata.region` is `bahrain` on 26/27. The Arabic findings rest on code paths (no locale plumbing anywhere) and on the lane's constructed 58-query Arabic slice, not on traffic.
14. **On-device rendering was never observed.** No bidi/RTL reordering of a mixed Arabic-label + Latin-value price line, no Arabic-Indic digit behaviour under Hermes. Finding `-10` is measured under node ICU and reasoned for Hermes.
15. **~700 of the 2,241 verdict-prefix tokens come from data files nobody read line by line** — the verdict exemplars, the three survey-derived pain workflows and the cohort priors. That is the prompts lane's own largest self-declared gap.
16. **GitHub issue bodies were unreadable** (the GitHub MCP server failed to connect: `Authorization header is badly formatted`), so dedupe against `#64` / `#66` / `#68` / `#80` is **by title only**. Three findings flag that they may overlap.
17. **Whether `76ace90` is still what prod runs** was taken from the brief, not re-checked by these lanes; the shared clone had already moved to `7dd04c1` with seven dark-flag fix commits, none of whose flags appear in the Railway variable list.
