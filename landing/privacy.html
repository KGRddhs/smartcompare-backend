Privacy Policy

MYEZ

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Changes on October 29, 2026: we added Anthropic as one of our AI providers (section 4), our part in OpenAI's data-sharing programme ended on that date (section 4), and the App will ask again for your permission to send your requests to our AI providers.

1. Who we are

MYEZ (the "App") is provided by Husain Aseeri, an individual app developer in the Kingdom of Bahrain, trading as MYEZ ("we", "us"). Postal address: Kingdom of Bahrain (our postal address is available on request at privacy@getmyez.com). We decide how and why your personal data is processed in the App. Contact: privacy@getmyez.com.

This policy explains what data we collect, why, who receives it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it.

2. Summary

MYEZ compares two products for you. To do that we process the products you enter or photograph, your account details and the preferences you choose to give us. MYEZ uses AI providers to process your requests: Anthropic, PBC and OpenAI, L.L.C. We are moving this processing to Anthropic; until the move is complete, some or all requests, including photos, are processed by OpenAI. We use AI to identify products and write comparisons (section 4), and we search retailer websites for prices. We do not sell or otherwise exchange your personal data for anything of value, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers. We took part in OpenAI's data-sharing programme, under which OpenAI could use the inputs we sent it and the outputs it generated from them to evaluate and train its models in return for some free use of its service; our part in it has ended (section 4). We do not show third-party ads and we do not track you across other companies' apps. We may share or sell aggregated or anonymised comparison and preference statistics (for example price trends or category popularity) that cannot identify you.

3. What we collect and why

3.1 Your account

  • Email address and password: required to create an account and sign in. Your password is stored only by our sign-in provider, in hashed form.
  • Display name: optional, shown in your profile.
  • Sign-in method: if you use Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, we record which one you used. What Apple and Google share with us is described in section 5.
  • Acceptance records: when you accepted the Terms of Service, the version you saw, and when you confirmed your age.

3.2 Your comparisons

  • What you compare: the product names you type, the links you paste (and the text of those web pages), and the photos you take or pick. We do not store photos; section 4 explains what is sent to our AI providers.
  • Comparison results: each comparison and its result, saved to your history until you delete it.
  • Search records: what you searched for, whether the search worked, how long it took and what it cost us, linked to your account when you are signed in.
  • Feedback and messages: the feedback you give on a comparison, the messages you send us through Contact Us and the email you send to our addresses.

3.3 Your preferences and optional answers

  • Preferences: the priorities, budget level, lifestyle tags and brand attitude you choose, and your notification settings. We keep a history of changes to your preferences.
  • Optional profile answers: your age group, gender and area, if you choose to give them, and your language and country, which you choose during onboarding or which we may set from your device's language and your network location.
  • How you heard about us: your answer, if you give one.
  • Behaviour profile: a summary we derive from how you use comparisons (for example which sections you read and for how long). We use it to adjust how comparisons are scored for you; we do not send it to our AI providers.

3.4 Your device and use of the App

  • Device identifier: a one-way code made from your device and a random value. We use it to stop abuse of the free limits and of invites.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, the token that lets us send them to your device.
  • Notification records: which reminders we sent you, when, and their text.
  • In-app activity: for example which screens and sections you open and for how long.
  • Usage and referral records: how many comparisons you have used, your invite code, the invites you sent or used, and the rewards they earned.
  • Error and performance reports: see section 13.

3.5 Security records

  • Sign-in and security events: for example sign-ins, failed sign-in attempts and checks for invite abuse. These records can include an IP address. Records of repeated failed sign-ins keep a one-way code of the email address that was tried.

3.6 Why we use your data

  • To provide the App under our agreement with you (the Terms of Service): your account, your comparisons (including sending them to our AI providers to produce the result), your history and the security of the App.
  • With your consent: your optional profile answers, notifications and your answer about how you heard about us. You can withdraw your consent at any time (section 10).
  • The permission the App asks for before your first comparison is the permission Apple requires before an app shares data with a third-party AI service (section 4).
  • In our legitimate interest in improving and funding the service: producing de-identified statistics about comparisons and preferences.

Your email address and a password are required to create an account; without them you cannot create one. Everything else in section 3.3, and notifications, are optional; without them comparisons are less personalised.

4. Comparisons made with AI (Anthropic and OpenAI)

MYEZ uses AI providers to process your requests: Anthropic, PBC and OpenAI, L.L.C. We are moving this processing to Anthropic; until the move is complete, some or all requests, including photos, are processed by OpenAI.

To identify products and write your comparison, we send our AI providers the product names you type, the links you paste together with the text of those web pages, and the photos you take or pick. We may also send the preferences on your profile, such as your priorities, budget level, lifestyle tags and brand attitude; your country, language and area; and general patterns from people whose survey answers are similar to yours (we never send your age group or gender themselves). We do not send your name, email address or account identifiers.

Before your first comparison or scan, the App asks your permission to send this information to our AI providers. This is the permission Apple requires before an app shares data with a third-party AI service. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account in the App (Profile, then the settings icon, then Delete account), or by emailing privacy@getmyez.com and asking us to delete your account; without it the App cannot run comparisons.

Anthropic, PBC processes the information we send it to provide its service to us under its commercial terms, and it does not use that information to train its models. Anthropic keeps the information it receives for up to 30 days, and requests we send in batches, with their results, for up to 29 days, unless it must keep them longer to enforce its usage policies or to comply with the law.

If we use Anthropic's web search to look up product information, Anthropic's search provider receives the search terms, such as product names, and never your identity.

Until the move to Anthropic is complete, OpenAI processes some or all requests, including photos, and receives the same kinds of information described above. We took part in OpenAI's data-sharing programme, which let OpenAI use the inputs we sent it and the outputs it generated from them to evaluate and train its models in return for some free use of its service; our part in the programme ended on October 29, 2026. Since then, OpenAI does not use the data we send it to train its models, and it may keep that data in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days. Data shared with OpenAI under the programme before that date remains governed by OpenAI's terms.

Please photograph products only, and do not include people, documents, payment cards or other personal or health details in your photos or searches.

Comparisons are generated by AI and can be wrong; see our Terms of Service.

5. Who receives your data

  • Anthropic: processes the requests we send it to identify products and write comparisons (section 4).
  • OpenAI: processes some or all requests, including photos, until our move to Anthropic is complete (section 4).
  • Apple: only if you use Sign in with Apple. Apple shares your email address, or a private relay address, with us. Apple also delivers notifications to iPhones.
  • Google: if you use Google Sign-In, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us through its sign-in token.
  • Our database and sign-in provider: stores your account and the records described in section 3, and sends sign-in emails such as password resets, directly or through an email-delivery provider.
  • Our hosting provider: runs our servers. Its logs can record each request to our servers, including the IP address it came from and the web address requested, which for a comparison can include the product names.
  • Our temporary-storage provider: holds usage counters, sign-in security checks and short-lived copies of data that make the App faster.
  • Our error-reporting provider: receives error and performance reports from our servers and from the App (section 13).
  • Our notification and update provider: delivers notifications (through Apple's notification service) and App updates. It receives your notification token and the text of each notification, and your device's requests for updates.
  • Web search and page-retrieval providers: receive product search terms and retailer page addresses, never your identity.
  • Our email providers: an email-routing service receives the email you send to our addresses and forwards it to our mailbox provider, which stores it under its own terms.
  • The retailer websites and store search services we query: see section 6.

Except as section 4 describes for data shared with OpenAI before October 29, 2026 and as this section describes for our mailbox provider, each of our service providers processes data only to provide its service to us, and we require each of them to protect it at least as well as this policy does. Retailer websites (section 6), the sign-in providers Apple and Google, our mailbox provider and the web-font service our website uses act under their own terms. We will tell you the names of our service providers on request at privacy@getmyez.com. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

People you send a comparison or invite link to can see that comparison, without the parts personalised for you. People who open an invite link you share can also see your display name. If you joined through a friend's invite, the notification we send that friend can show your display name.

When you visit our website, our hosting provider can log your request, including your IP address, and the pages load fonts from a web-font service, which receives your IP address.

6. Retailer websites

To find prices we search public retailer websites and their search services for the products in your comparison. Those requests come from our servers and contain only product search terms; they do not include your identity. For comparisons by link, our servers open the page addresses you paste. Product pictures in the App are loaded directly from the retailer or image website that published them, or from a search engine's image server, so the website that serves each picture receives your device's IP address and the standard details your device sends with any web request.

We may also keep our own catalogue of product information (product names, sizes and prices, and the web addresses of product pictures and of the pages they came from) so that later comparisons are faster and more complete. When we run it, our catalogue crawler, identified by the user-agent string published at https://getmyez.com/bot/, reads merchant pages whose robots.txt permits it. We may also add product details that our team enters by hand from pages it visits in the usual way, product feeds and data that partners license to us, and product details we find during comparisons. The catalogue holds product information only, never who compared a product.

Stores may pay to show a link to their website or shop next to a product; such a link is marked "Sponsored". Payment never changes how products are compared, scored or ranked. A tap on a store link may be recorded with your other in-App activity (section 3.4).

7. Transfers outside Bahrain

Our providers process data outside Bahrain, including in the United States and Germany (European Union), as well as in India and Singapore. We transfer data to our service providers, which are required by contract to protect it, and to our mailbox provider, which handles email under its own terms (section 5).

8. How long we keep data

  • Your account, profile, preferences, optional answers, comparisons, history, feedback, messages and activity records linked to your account: until you delete them or your account; section 9 describes what deleting your account removes.
  • Usage counters: daily and monthly counters in temporary storage expire within about 32 days. Your total number of comparisons stays with your account; section 9 describes what deleting your account removes.
  • Sign-in security records linked to your account, which can include IP addresses: as long as your account exists; section 9 explains what happens to them when you delete it.
  • Records of failed sign-ins and lockouts, which are not linked to an account and include IP addresses: no fixed period.
  • Searches and activity records that are not linked to an account: no fixed period.
  • Email you send to our addresses: kept in our mailbox until we delete it; no fixed period.
  • Photos: we do not store them; section 4 explains what our AI providers keep.
  • Short-lived security checks and copies in temporary storage: expire within 6 hours.
  • Backups and service logs: copies in our providers' backups and logs are deleted on their schedules.

9. Deleting your account

When you delete your account (Profile, then the settings icon, then Delete account), we immediately and permanently delete from our servers your comparisons and the links you shared, your searches, feedback, usage, referral and in-app activity records and your preference history, and we erase your email address, name, sign-in provider, demographic answers, referral code, notification token, device identifier, preferences, consent records and other profile details from your account record. After deletion we keep a de-identified record (a random account number, the date the account was created and the date it was erased), which is removed when we delete your sign-in account at the end of the deletion, and security logs linked to that number with your IP address removed: sign-in events, referral-abuse checks and, if you joined with an invite code, the record of that redemption (which keeps the inviter's code). Short-lived usage counters keyed by that number (how many comparisons were used today and this month) expire within about 32 days. Records of failed sign-in attempts and lockouts are not linked to your account and keep the IP address (and, for lockouts, a one-way hash of the email address) for no fixed period. If your account redeemed an invite, that invite and its redemption are removed too, including the bonus the inviter received from it. Copies in our backups and service logs (including our hosting, error-reporting and sign-in providers' logs) expire on those systems' schedules.

De-identified comparison data and statistics that cannot identify you may be kept after deletion. Email you sent to our addresses stays in our mailbox until we delete it; ask us at privacy@getmyez.com if you want it deleted too.

10. Your rights

  • Access and a copy: you can see your profile, preferences and comparison history in the App, and you can ask us for a copy of your data. We reply within 15 working days.
  • Correction: you can update your profile and preferences in the App, or ask us to correct your data.
  • Deletion, objection and restriction: you can ask us to delete your data, to stop processing it or to limit how we process it. We reply within 10 working days.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can withdraw your permission for AI processing by deleting your account in the App (Profile, then the settings icon, then Delete account), and any other consent by emailing us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it.
  • How to ask: email privacy@getmyez.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority of Bahrain (https://www.pdp.gov.bh) or, if you live in Saudi Arabia, to the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).

11. Notifications

If you allow notifications, we may send you referral updates (for example when a friend you invited completes their first comparison, or before a referral reward expires) and up to one reminder a week about your past comparisons. We do not send advertising. Apart from the reminders described above, we do not use your personal data for direct marketing, and we do not share it with others for their marketing. The notification switch in the App stops reminders; to stop all notifications, use your iPhone's Settings.

12. Children and young people

The App is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it. If you are under 18, or under the age of majority where you live if that is higher, a parent or guardian must agree to this policy and to our Terms of Service for you. The App tells you that comparisons are written by AI and asks your permission before your first comparison, and we check requests and results for unsafe content.

13. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and the App checks our server's identity before sending data. Your sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage.

Error reports are set up not to include your name, email or IP address, and we filter common credential formats out of them. From our servers, our error-reporting provider receives exception types, scrubbed message templates (which may include internal account and comparison identifiers), scrubbed request metadata (method, path, user agent) and the website host name of a product link you submit - not raw exception text, request bodies, credentials, or the content of your searches. Reports from the App can include the error message, your device model, operating-system and app versions, and a record of recent App activity, including the addresses of the App's requests to our servers, which for a comparison can include the product names; they are set up not to include your name, email or IP address. Performance traces from the App record the same request addresses. Performance traces from our servers record which AI model handled a request and how many tokens it used, not the text.

If a breach puts your data at risk, we will notify you and the competent authority as the law requires.

14. Data kept on your device

The App keeps your sign-in tokens, a random install identifier (used with your account for the free-comparison limits) and a second random identifier the App uses to choose which new features you see, all stored securely in your iPhone's Keychain, and your profile summary, recent searches, onboarding answers not yet sent, your AI permission record, language settings and a cached copy of this policy in app storage. Until 31 March 2027 the App also sends an older form of the install identifier, which combined that random identifier with your device's software version, so your free-comparison history carries over; after that date only the random install identifier is used. When the Keychain (the Keystore on Android) cannot be read, the App uses the identifier Apple or Google provides to app developers for that device instead (the vendor identifier on iPhone, the Android ID on Android), for the free-comparison limits only. Signing out removes the sign-in tokens and the profile summary; deleting your account also removes the AI permission record. Items in the Keychain can remain on the iPhone after the App is deleted.
