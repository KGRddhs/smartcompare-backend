1. Introduction

Qaren ("we", "our", "us") operates the Qaren mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and password when you create an account.
  • Preferences: Product comparison preferences including priorities, budget level, lifestyle tags, and brand attitude.
  • Feedback: Comparison feedback (thumbs up/down, suggestions) you voluntarily submit.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Product comparison queries, search history, event interactions (tab switches, source clicks, view durations).
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version.
  • Camera Data: Photos taken within the App for product identification are processed in real-time and not stored on our servers.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Social Login: If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and basic profile information from the respective provider. We do not receive or store your social media passwords.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and personalize product comparisons
  • Maintain your comparison history
  • Improve our comparison accuracy and service quality
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance the App
  • Communicate service updates (with your consent)
  • Detect and prevent misuse of the App

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting (Railway), database (Supabase), AI processing (OpenAI), search (Serper), caching (Upstash), and error tracking (Sentry) — only as necessary to operate the App.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, regulation, or legal process.

5. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active. Deleted upon account deletion request.
  • Comparison History: Retained while your account is active.
  • Anonymous Analytics: Aggregated, non-identifiable data may be retained indefinitely for service improvement.
  • Cached Data: Product prices cached for 24 hours; specs and reviews cached for 7 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View your personal data through the App.
  • Correction: Update your profile and preferences at any time.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data from within the App.
  • Export: Request a copy of your data by contacting us.
  • Withdraw Consent: Stop using the App at any time.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication with JWT tokens
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • Server-side input validation

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Regional Considerations

Qaren operates primarily in the GCC region (Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman). We comply with applicable data protection laws in these jurisdictions.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App or via email.

11. AI Quality Improvement Program

To make Qaren's comparisons sharper over time, we participate in OpenAI's Data Sharing Program.

What we share: your search queries; your country, language, and governorate; your stated preferences (budget tier, priorities, brand attitude, lifestyle tags); aggregate findings from users similar to you; AI-generated comparison responses.

What we never share: your name, email, phone, payment info, account ID, your specific age or gender, your individual comparison history or behavior data, or anything that ties data back to your personal identity.

Your right to opt out: Under Bahrain's PDPL, Saudi Arabia's PDPL, and similar GCC regulations, you can disable AI sharing in Settings → Privacy → "Help improve AI quality." When off, your queries are processed without contributing to AI evaluation. Comparisons still work — they just don't help train the system.

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@qaren.app
