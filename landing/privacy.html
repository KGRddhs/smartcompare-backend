2. Summary

MYEZ compares two products for you. To do that we process the products you enter or photograph, your account details and the preferences you choose to give us. We use OpenAI to identify products and write comparisons, and we search retailer websites for prices. We do not sell your personal data, we do not show third-party ads and we do not track you across other companies' apps.

3. What we collect and why

3.1 Your account

  • Email address and password: required to create an account and sign in. Your password is stored only by our sign-in provider, in hashed form.
  • Display name: optional, shown in your profile.
  • Sign-in method: if you use Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, we record which one you used. What Apple and Google share with us is described in section 5.
  • Acceptance records: when you accepted the Terms of Service, the version you saw, and when you confirmed your age.

3.2 Your comparisons

  • What you compare: the product names you type, the links you paste (and the text of those web pages), and the photos you take or pick. We do not store photos; section 4 explains what is sent to OpenAI.
  • Comparison results: each comparison and its result, saved to your history until you delete it.
  • Search records: what you searched for, whether the search worked, how long it took and what it cost us, linked to your account when you are signed in.
  • Feedback and messages: the feedback you give on a comparison and the messages you send us through Contact Us.

3.3 Your preferences and optional answers

  • Preferences: the priorities, budget level, lifestyle tags and brand attitude you choose, and your notification and sharing settings. We keep a history of changes to your preferences.
  • Optional profile answers: your age group, gender and area, if you choose to give them, and your language and country, which you choose during onboarding or which we may set from your device's language and your network location.
  • How you heard about us: your answer, if you give one.
  • Behaviour profile: a summary we derive from how you use comparisons (for example which sections you read and for how long). We use it to adjust how comparisons are scored for you; we do not send it to OpenAI.

3.4 Your device and use of the App

  • Device identifier: a one-way code made from your device and a random value. We use it to stop abuse of the free limits and of invites.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, the token that lets us send them to your device.
  • Notification records: which reminders we sent you, when, and their text.
  • In-app activity: for example which screens and sections you open and for how long.
  • Usage and referral records: how many comparisons you have used, your invite code, the invites you sent or used, and the rewards they earned.
  • Error and performance reports: see section 13.

3.5 Security records

  • Sign-in and security events: for example sign-ins, failed sign-in attempts and checks for invite abuse. These records can include an IP address. Records of repeated failed sign-ins keep a one-way code of the email address that was tried.

3.6 Why we use your data

  • To provide the App under our agreement with you (the Terms of Service): your account, your comparisons (including sending them to OpenAI to produce the result), your history and the security of the App.
  • With your consent: your optional profile answers, notifications and your answer about how you heard about us. You can withdraw your consent at any time (section 10).
  • The permission the App asks for before your first comparison is the permission Apple requires before an app shares data with a third-party AI service (section 4).

Your email address and a password are required to create an account; without them you cannot create one. Everything else in section 3.3, and notifications, are optional; without them comparisons are less personalised.

4. Comparisons made with AI (OpenAI)

To identify products and write your comparison, we send OpenAI the product names you type, the links you paste together with the text of those web pages, and the photos you take or pick. We may also send the preferences on your profile, such as your priorities, budget level, lifestyle tags and brand attitude; your country, language and area; and general patterns from people whose survey answers are similar to yours (we never send your age group or gender themselves). We do not send your name, email address or account identifiers.

Before your first comparison or scan, the App asks your permission to send this information to OpenAI. This is the permission Apple requires before an app shares data with a third-party AI service. You can withdraw it at any time in <PLACEHOLDER:WITHDRAW_PATH>; without it the App cannot run comparisons.

OpenAI does not use data sent to its API to train its models unless the developer opts in, and we have not opted in. OpenAI may keep data sent to its API in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days.

Comparisons are generated by AI and can be wrong; see our Terms of Service.

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in service. It stores your account and the records described in section 3, and sends sign-in emails such as password resets.

  • Google: only if you use Google Sign-In. Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us through its sign-in token.

Each provider processes data only to provide its service to us, and we require each to protect it at least as well as this policy does. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

People you send a comparison or invite link to can see that comparison, without the parts personalised for you. People who open an invite link you share can also see your display name. If you joined through a friend's invite, the notification we send that friend can show your display name.

6. Retailer websites

To find prices we search public retailer websites and their search services for the products in your comparison. Those requests come from our servers and contain only product search terms; they do not include your identity. For comparisons by link, our servers open the page addresses you paste. Product pictures in the App are loaded directly from the retailer or image website that published them, or from a search engine's image server, so the website that serves each picture receives your device's IP address and the standard details your device sends with any web request.

13. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and the App checks our server's certificate before sending data (certificate pinning). Your sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage (Keychain). Administrative functions require a secret key.

Error reports are set up not to include your name, email or IP address, and we filter common credential formats out of them. From our servers, Sentry receives exception types, scrubbed message templates (which may include internal account and comparison identifiers), scrubbed request metadata (method, path, user agent) and the website host name of a product link you submit - not raw exception text, request bodies, credentials, or the content of your searches. Reports from the App can include the error message, your device model, operating-system and app versions, and a record of recent App activity, including the addresses of the App's requests to our servers, which for a comparison can include the product names; they are set up not to include your name, email or IP address. Performance traces from the App record the same request addresses. Performance traces from our servers record which AI model handled a request and how many tokens it used, not the text.

If a breach puts your data at risk, we will notify you and the competent authority as the law requires.

14. Data kept on your device

The App keeps sign-in tokens and two random device identifiers in your iPhone's secure storage (Keychain), and your profile summary, recent searches, onboarding answers not yet sent, your AI permission record, language settings and a cached copy of this policy in app storage. Signing out removes the sign-in tokens and the profile summary; deleting your account also removes the AI permission record. Items in the Keychain can remain on the iPhone after the App is deleted.
